About

Purple Cow Investment Partners invests in founder- and family-owned companies across the Midwest, Mountain West and Texas: businesses that stand out for strong leadership and disciplined processes. We partner with owners to grow what they have built, with structures that let them share in the upside.

Unlike traditional private equity, we don’t lead with cost cutting or sweeping changes; we focus on strategic growth around shared values. Growth is led by the existing team, not roll-ups. We help build management depth and professionalize pricing and procurement, and we live and invest in the communities we serve. We know how to build a business because we have built our own.
